How it's calculated:
1. Evaporation rate — Penman-Monteith simplified:
E ≈ 6.43 × (1 + 0.536·u) × VPD / λ   [mm/hr]
where VPD = vapour pressure deficit, u = wind speed, λ = latent heat of vaporisation (2.45 MJ/kg)

2. Cooling power — Q = E × A_stream × ρ_water × λ   [W]

3. Park-wide temp reduction — ΔT_park = Q / (A_park × h_conv)
where h_conv ≈ 10–25 W/m²·K (convective heat transfer coefficient)

4. Near-stream bonus — An additional 1–4 °C within ~5–20m of the stream from direct evaporative + radiative effects. Scales with stream width and local humidity deficit.

Model uses simplified Penman-Monteith evaporation with a turbulent convection layer.
Real values vary with shading, soil moisture, vegetation, and albedo.
Typical measured values: 2–8 °C cooler near urban water features.
